Important - Filename Format Required:
Your filename MUST follow this format:
CourseCode_CourseName_InstructorName.xlsx

Example: BCS201_LogicForComputerScience_DrHamza.xlsx

Format Rules:
• CourseCode: Letters and numbers (e.g., BCS201, ENG102)
• CourseName: Course name with underscores (e.g., LogicForComputerScience)
• InstructorName: Your name (e.g., DrHamza, MehakKhurana)
• Use underscores (_) to separate each part
• Do NOT use spaces in the filename
